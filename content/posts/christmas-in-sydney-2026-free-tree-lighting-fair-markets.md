Your first Christmas in Australia? Sydney's free festive events start in late November 2026.

## Martin Place Christmas Tree (CBD)

The official lighting ceremony is on Thu 26 Nov. Sound and light shows then run nightly until 5 January.

## Glebe Christmas Fair

Sat 28 Nov, 12pm–8pm on Glebe Point Road (between St Johns Road and Great Western Highway).

## Martin Place Christmas Markets

Open 29 and 30 November from 11am to 9:15pm, then on selected days until Christmas Eve. Sources list different days, so check the City of Sydney listing before you go.

## More free events in December

- Choirs in the City: carols daily from 1 to 21 December, 6pm–8pm.
- Free local Christmas concerts in Rosebery (29 Nov), Pyrmont (6 Dec) and Alexandria (13 Dec).
