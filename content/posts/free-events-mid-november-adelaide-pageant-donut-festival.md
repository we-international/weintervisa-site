Mid-November has some great free events. Here are two worth planning around.

## Adelaide Christmas Pageant (Adelaide)

Sat 14 Nov 2026 in Adelaide's city centre. Free. Check [christmaspageant.com.au](https://www.christmaspageant.com.au) for the route and start time.

## Head On Photo Festival (Sydney)

On until 29 Nov 2026. Free outdoor photography along the Bondi Beach promenade, at Bondi Pavilion Gallery, Paddington Reservoir Gardens and in the CBD. Details at [headon.org.au](https://headon.org.au).

_Update (9 Nov 2026): an earlier version of this post listed the Melbourne Donut Festival at Queen Victoria Market for 17–18 November. The 2026 festival was held on 17–18 October, so we've removed it._

## Before you go

- Times can change, so check the organiser's page before you go.
- Big events get crowded. Plan your public transport home.
