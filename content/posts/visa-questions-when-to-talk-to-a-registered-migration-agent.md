We help students compare courses and enrol. But we're an education agency, not a migration agent, so we don't give visa advice. Here's when it makes sense to talk to a registered migration agent.

## When to get professional visa advice

- You want to change visas, for example from a Working Holiday visa to a Student visa.
- Your visa is close to expiring, or you're not sure which conditions apply to you.
- You've had a visa refused or cancelled, or you've received a letter from Home Affairs.
- Your situation is complicated (family members, previous visas, health or character questions).

## Recent changes to keep in mind

From 2 October 2026, most temporary visa holders, including Working Holiday Makers, can no longer apply for a Student visa while in Australia. Read our summary: [Student visa changes from 2 October 2026](https://www.weintervisa.com/post/student-visa-changes-from-2-october-2026-what-whms-need-to-know).

## Check before you choose an adviser

In Australia, people who give immigration advice must be registered migration agents (or Australian legal practitioners). You can check a migration agent's registration on the [Office of the Migration Agents Registration Authority (OMARA) register](https://portal.mara.gov.au/search-the-register-of-migration-agents/).
