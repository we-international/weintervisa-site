Interested in working in aged care or disability support? The **CHC33021 Certificate III in Individual Support** is a nationally recognised entry-level qualification for this field in Australia.

## What the course covers

Providers such as TAFE NSW describe the qualification as **15 units — 9 core and 6 specialty**. Core topics include:

- Providing individualised, person-centred support
- Supporting independence and wellbeing
- Infection prevention and safe work practices
- Communicating in health and community services
- Working legally and ethically, and with diverse people

Specialty units usually focus on ageing, disability, dementia care or community participation, depending on the stream you choose.

## Work placement

The qualification includes a **mandatory work placement** — TAFE NSW, for example, lists **120 hours** with a suitable industry employer. Placement is where you practise real care skills under supervision, so plan your time for it.

## Job roles it can lead to

According to TAFE NSW, graduates work in roles such as aged care worker, home and community care worker, disability support worker, NDIS worker and residential support worker. Employment is never guaranteed — it depends on your skills, availability and employers' requirements.
