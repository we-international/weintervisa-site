Moving to a new city to study? A homestay means living with a local host in their home. It can make your first weeks in Australia easier while you settle in.

For prices, steps and FAQs, see our [Homestay in Australia page](https://www.weintervisa.com/homestay).

## What a homestay usually includes

- A furnished private room in the host's home.
- Some packages include meals. Check what's included before you book.
- A local contact who can help you get to know the area.

## Questions to ask before you book

- How far is it from your campus, and what public transport is nearby?
- What's included in the weekly price (meals, internet, bills)?
- House rules: guests, cooking, quiet hours.
- How much notice do you need to give if you want to leave?
