The weekend of 20–22 November 2026 brings free cultural festivals to Melbourne and Sydney.

## African Music & Cultural Festival (Melbourne)

20–22 Nov at Federation Square. Free, according to Melbourne's November events guide.

## Cachivaches Latin Market (Melbourne)

21–22 Nov at Queen Victoria Market. Free entry.

## Philippine Christmas Festival (Sydney)

21–22 Nov at Tumbalong Park, Darling Harbour. Free, according to the Sydney Expert November guide.

## Before you go

- Programs and times can change, so check the organiser's page before you go.
- Entry is free, but food and market stalls are paid.
