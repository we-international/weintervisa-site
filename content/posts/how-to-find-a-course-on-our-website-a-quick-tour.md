Planning to study in Australia, or thinking about a short course while you travel? Our website brings the main steps together in one place. Here's a quick tour of the pages that help most, and what to do on each one.

## 1. Explore Courses: search by name, code or city

Our [Explore Courses](https://www.weintervisa.com/courses) page lists course families across many study areas, from community services and health to hospitality, trades and English. You can:

- search by course name or national course code (for example CHC33021)
- filter by subject area and city
- open a course card to see what you'll learn, the approximate duration, possible career directions and campus options

Fees are not shown publicly because they change by school, campus and intake. When you find something you like, send us an enquiry and we'll check current fees and next start dates for you.

## 2. Fast-track Courses: for Working Holiday visa holders

Holding a Working Holiday visa (subclass 417 or 462)? Our [Fast-track Courses](https://www.weintervisa.com/fast-track-courses) page shows the Certificate III courses we help Working Holiday Makers with: Individual Support, Health Services Assistance, and Early Childhood Education and Care. Weeks, timetables and intakes vary by school.

Visa conditions apply to every Working Holiday visa, including limits on study. Check your own visa conditions in [VEVO](https://www.weintervisa.com/post/how-to-check-your-australian-visa-for-free-with-vevo) before you enrol.

## 3. OSHC: compare student health cover

If you plan to study on a student visa, you'll usually need Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for your stay. Our [OSHC page](https://www.weintervisa.com/oshc) lets you compare and buy cover from partner insurers online. Policy questions go to the insurer, so read the policy details before you buy.

## 4. Migration Support: referral for visa advice

We're an education agency working with registered migration partners, and we don't give visa advice ourselves. If you need advice about your visa situation, our [Migration Support](https://www.weintervisa.com/migration-support) page explains how we can refer you to a migration professional.

## 5. Blog: guides and tips

Our [blog](https://www.weintervisa.com/blog) has plain-English guides on courses, Working Holiday rules from official sources, cheap eats, day trips and events around Australia.

## Tips before you enquire

- Have your visa type and expiry date ready (check VEVO)
- Know which city you'd like to live in
- Think about your budget and when you'd like to start
- Never share passport or bank details in public comments

## Questions? Send us an enquiry

Fill in our enquiry form at [weintervisa.com/contact-us](https://www.weintervisa.com/contact-us) and tell us your goals, budget and timing. We'll come back to you with course options, fees and next intakes. We can't guarantee admission, jobs or visa outcomes.
