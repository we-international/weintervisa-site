Melbourne is famous for food — and you don't need a big budget to enjoy it. Here are three CBD lunch spots where a meal usually comes in under $15, based on RMIT University's student cheap-eats guide (March 2026).

## 1. Udon Yasan — Bourke Street

Quick, hot and filling udon noodle bowls, typically under $10. A great warm-up on a cold Melbourne day.

## 2. VB Rolls — Bourke Street

Vietnamese bánh mì and rice paper rolls for around $10–12. Easy to take away and eat on the go.

## 3. Goz City — QV Melbourne

Turkish pide (flatbread) under $10, with vegetarian options around $6–8.

## Extra snacks under $5

- Tokui Sushi (Lonsdale Street) — sushi rolls from about $2.
- Breadtop (QV Melbourne) — buns and pastries around $3–5.

## Tips for eating cheap in the city

- Travel around the CBD for free inside the Free Tram Zone.
- Lunch is often better value than dinner — look for lunch specials.
- Prices and opening hours change, so check before you go.
