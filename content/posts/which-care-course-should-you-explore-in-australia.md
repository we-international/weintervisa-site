Choosing a care course starts with the work you want to do and the practical training you can commit to. Here are three qualifications our team can help you compare.

## Individual Support

**CHC33021 Certificate III in Individual Support** is a route to explore if you are interested in supporting older people or people with disability. Ask about the chosen specialisation, work placement, timetable and the full time to completion.

## Health Services Assistance

**HLT33115 Certificate III in Health Services Assistance** is worth comparing if you are interested in a support role in a healthcare setting. Check the exact course scope, practical requirements, placement and entry conditions before choosing a provider.

## Early Childhood Education and Care

**CHC30125 Certificate III in Early Childhood Education and Care** is a route to explore for work with young children. Confirm the current qualification code, placement arrangements, class location and required checks with each college.

## Compare the complete offer

For each option, ask for the campus, eligible student group, intake, delivery schedule, tuition, enrolment and material fees, placement requirements and price validity. A listed tuition figure alone may not be the full cost. Course availability and suitability vary by provider and by student.
