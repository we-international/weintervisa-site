Holding a Working Holiday visa (subclass 417 or 462) and interested in care work? **CHC33021 Certificate III in Individual Support** is one of our Fast-track courses. One question we hear a lot is: what's the difference between the Ageing, Disability, and Ageing and Disability versions? Here's a plain-English guide based on the national training package.

## What the qualification covers

According to training.gov.au, CHC33021 is for people who provide person-centred support in community, home or residential care, following an individualised plan and working under supervision in a team. People may need support because of ageing, disability or other reasons.

The packaging rules (Release 1) are:

- **15 units in total:** 9 core units and 6 electives
- at least 3 electives must come from Group A (Ageing) or Group B (Disability)
- at least **120 hours of work**, as set out in the assessment requirements

## The three specialisations

- **Ageing:** includes all Group A electives (CHCAGE011, CHCAGE013 and CHCPAL003), covering support for older people, including people living with dementia and people receiving palliative care.
- **Disability:** includes all Group B electives (CHCDIS011, CHCDIS012 and CHCDIS020), covering community participation, independence and person-centred support for people with disability.
- **Ageing and Disability:** includes all six Group A and Group B electives, so it covers both areas.

Which ones are offered depends on the school, so ask which specialisation is on your offer.

## Which one suits you?

- Prefer working with older people in residential aged care or home care? **Ageing** may suit you.
- Interested in supporting people with disability at home or in the community? **Disability** may suit you.
- Want to keep both doors open? The combined **Ageing and Disability** option covers both.
