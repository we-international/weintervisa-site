Sydney's **Thaitown** in Haymarket is one of the best areas in the city for tasty food that won't blow your budget — and it's a short walk from Central Station.

## 1. Chat Thai — 20 Campbell St, Haymarket

A long-running Sydney favourite known for its big menu, from curries and som tum (papaya salad) to grilled pork, plus a late-night supper menu.

## 2. Boon Cafe — 425 Pitt St, Haymarket

Known for Thai-style breakfast and bakes during the day, with Isaan-style dishes later. Good for a relaxed brunch.

## 3. Thanon Khao San — 413 Pitt St, Haymarket

Named after Bangkok's famous backpacker street, with Thai street-food style dishes and desserts.

## Tips

- Opening hours and menus change — check each venue's latest hours before you go.
- Peak dinner times get busy; go early or late to avoid long waits.
- While you're there, Thaitown's grocery stores are handy for cheap ingredients if you cook at home.
