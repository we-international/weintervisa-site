Not sure exactly what your visa allows? **VEVO (Visa Entitlement Verification Online)** is the Department of Home Affairs' online service for checking visa details and conditions.

## What VEVO shows you

- Your visa type and grant details
- Your visa expiry date
- Your visa conditions — for example, work or study limits

Home Affairs also explains that you can **send proof of your visa conditions to others** — for example employers, landlords or the country you'd like to visit.

## How to use it

1. Go to the official [Home Affairs VEVO page](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/already-have-a-visa/check-visa-details-and-conditions/check-conditions-online).
2. Choose the option for visa holders.
3. Enter the details it asks for, such as your passport information and a visa reference number from your grant letter.
4. Check your conditions and, if you need to, email the result to your employer.

## Stay safe

- **Only use the official .gov.au website.** Some third-party sites look official but aren't.
- Never share your passport number or visa grant number in public comments or social media.

## Working Holiday Makers: two conditions to know

If you're on a 417 or 462 visa, Home Affairs lists two conditions many people check first: **8547** (a maximum of 6 months' work with any one employer, unless you get permission) and **8548** (a maximum of 4 months of study or training).
