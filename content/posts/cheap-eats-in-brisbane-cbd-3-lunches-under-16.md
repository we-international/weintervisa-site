Working or studying in Brisbane on a budget? These three CBD lunch spots appear in Brisbane's official dining guide (May 2026) and usually cost less than $16.

## 1. Govinda's Pure Vegetarian (George Street)

A Brisbane favourite for filling vegetarian food. The guide lists a regular feast meal for **$12**.

## 2. Steamed (Turbot Street)

A good pick if you love dumplings. Mix and match **six dumplings and one bun for $12**.

## 3. Vege Rama (Post Office Square)

Quick, healthy and handy for office workers. A **medium hot dish and salad combo costs $15.90**.

## More cheap options

The same guide also mentions The Bagel Boys on George Street (chipotle chicken bagel, $12.50) and the Brisbane City Markets on Queen Street, with some options under $10. Time Out Brisbane (January 2026) adds Roti Place in the CBD, with roti from $12.40, and Vietnam House in Fortitude Valley, with lunch noodle and rice dishes from $13.

## Getting around for less

- The **Spring Hill Loop** and **City Loop** buses are free. The City Loop runs every 10 minutes on weekdays.
- Translink fares are a flat 50 cents across all zones and modes.

## Tips for eating well on a budget

- **Go at lunch.** Many city spots offer lunch deals that cost less than the same dish at dinner.
- **Share and mix.** Dumplings, rotis and rice dishes are easy to share, so you can try more for less.
- **Carry a water bottle.** Free water is easy to find, and you'll skip $4–5 bottles.
- **Plan your week.** Pick one or two cheap lunches out and cook the rest. Your savings add up quickly over a Working Holiday.
- **Follow your favourites.** Many small venues post daily specials on social media.

## Before you go

Prices and opening hours change, so check the venue's own page or social media before you head out.
