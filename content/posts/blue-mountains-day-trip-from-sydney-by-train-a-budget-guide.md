The Blue Mountains are one of the easiest big nature trips from Sydney — and you don't need a car. Here's how to do it in a day on a budget.

## Getting there by train

Trains run on the **Blue Mountains Line** from Central Station (also stopping at Strathfield, Parramatta, Blacktown and Penrith). According to Visit NSW, express trains take about **90 minutes** to Katoomba, and all-stations trains up to about **2 hours**.

- Pay with an **Opal card** or tap on and off with your **contactless credit or debit card**.
- Check live times on [Transport NSW](https://transportnsw.info/) before you leave — weekend trackwork can change services.

## Getting around Katoomba

From Katoomba station you can take local buses or the hop-on hop-off **Blue Mountains Explorer Bus**, which loops around Katoomba and Leura to the main lookouts. The most famous stop is **Echo Point**, where you'll see the Three Sisters rock formation.

## A simple one-day plan

1. Catch an early train from Central.
2. Head to Echo Point for the Three Sisters views.
3. Walk part of a cliff-top track (pick one that matches your fitness and the weather).
4. Grab lunch in Katoomba or Leura village.
5. Train back to Sydney in the late afternoon.

## Tips

- It's usually cooler in the mountains than in the city — bring a jumper and rain jacket.
- Wear proper walking shoes; some tracks have steep stairs.
- Carry water and check track closures on the NSW National Parks website before you go.

Source: [Visit NSW — Getting to the Blue Mountains](https://www.visitnsw.com/destinations/blue-mountains/getting-to-the-blue-mountains)
