On 17 November 2026, the Australian Government announced changes to how people apply for a second or third Working Holiday Maker (WHM) visa (subclass 417 or 462). Here is a plain summary of what the Department of Home Affairs and the Minister have published. This is general information only, not migration advice.

## What's changing

- A new registration process opens on 1 December 2026. You register your interest, and the government sends invitations to apply.

- From 1 March 2027, people seeking a second or third WHM visa (except UK citizens) must be invited to apply. The invitation gives a "Next Year Regional Work Guarantee".

- The Minister's release says registrations stay valid until 30 June 2027, the first monthly invitation round is expected in mid-January 2027, and an invitation lasts 12 months.

- You can register before you finish your specified work, according to Home Affairs.

## Specified work: 3 months becomes 4 for a second visa

- Second visa: the specified work requirement rises from three to four months from 1 March 2027, where it applies. Work completed before that date counts toward the new requirement.

- Third visa: stays at six months.

- The Minister's release lists 45,000 second-visa places and 25,000 third-visa places for 2026–27.

## If you're already on a Working Holiday visa

Home Affairs says you can still apply directly, without registering or being invited, if you complete your specified work and lodge a valid application by 28 February 2027, and meet all other eligibility requirements. Home Affairs says it will publish registration instructions before 1 December 2026.
