From 2 October 2026, Australia changed the rules for Student visa (subclass 500) applications. The biggest change for many of our readers: most temporary visa holders, including Working Holiday Makers, can no longer apply for a Student visa while they are in Australia. Here's a plain-English summary of what the official sources say.

## What changed on 2 October 2026

- In-country applications: according to [Study Australia's October 2026 update](https://www.studyaustralia.gov.au/en/tools-and-resources/news/student-visa-changes-october-2026), most temporary visa holders can no longer apply for a Student visa from inside Australia. It says this includes Working Holiday Maker and Skills in Demand visa holders.
- Exemptions listed by Study Australia include DFAT- or Defence-sponsored students, doctoral study, primary or secondary school study, provider default, up to 12 extra months to finish a course with the same provider, and progressing to a higher-level course after finishing the main course.
- Family members: in most cases, family members can't be added to a new Student visa application, and they can't be added after a Student visa is granted. Some groups are exempt.
- Current Student visas: Study Australia says the changes do not affect Student visas that have already been granted.
- Changing providers: a new Transfer stream within the Student visa is planned. Details have not been released yet.

## What it means if you're on a Working Holiday visa

If you hold a Working Holiday visa (subclass 417 or 462) and you're planning to move onto a Student visa, the official guidance says you would generally need to apply from outside Australia. Factor this into your timing, travel and budget, and check the latest rules before you make plans.

Studying while you're still on your Working Holiday visa is a separate question. Home Affairs sets the study limit for Working Holiday Makers through visa condition 8548 (up to 4 months of study or training). See [Home Affairs: Working Holiday Maker work and study conditions](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/programs-subsite/Pages/working-holiday-maker/work-conditions.aspx) and check your own conditions in VEVO.
