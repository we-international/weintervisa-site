Got sick or hurt on your Working Holiday? Depending on your country, you might be able to use Medicare, Australia's public health system, for some medically necessary care. This is thanks to **Reciprocal Health Care Agreements (RHCAs)**. Here's a quick, general overview from Services Australia information.

## Which countries have an agreement?

Australia has RHCAs with 11 countries:

- Belgium, Finland, Ireland, Italy, Malta, the Netherlands, New Zealand, Norway, Slovenia, Sweden and the United Kingdom

Some have special conditions. For example, visitors from Italy and Malta are generally covered for 6 months from each arrival, and UK visitors must be ordinarily resident in the UK.

## What can be covered?

For most RHCA countries, eligible visitors can get:

- medically necessary out-of-hospital care (for example a GP visit)
- medically necessary care as a public patient in a public hospital
- some medicines under the Pharmaceutical Benefits Scheme (PBS)

Visitors from **Ireland and New Zealand** aren't enrolled in Medicare: they can get public hospital care and some PBS medicines only.

## What isn't covered?

RHCAs don't cover everything. Examples that aren't covered include:

- ambulance travel
- treatment as a private patient or in a private hospital
- most allied health (like physio), glasses, hearing aids and cosmetic surgery
- treatment you planned before you arrived, or travel to Australia for treatment

That's why many travellers still choose private overseas visitor or travel insurance. Compare policies carefully and check what they include.

## How to enrol

Visitors from the UK and Italy can apply online, and visitors from other eligible countries use the Medicare enrolment form. You'll need your passport and visa details, and Services Australia checks your visa in VEVO. Eligible visitors get a yellow Medicare card. New to VEVO? If you later apply for a student visa, you'll usually need Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for your stay. You can compare and buy cover on our [OSHC page](https://www.weintervisa.com/oshc).
