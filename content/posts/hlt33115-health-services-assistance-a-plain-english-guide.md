HLT33115 Certificate III in Health Services Assistance is a national qualification for people who want to support nurses and other health professionals. It's one of our three [Fast-track courses](https://www.weintervisa.com/fast-track-courses). Course length is set by each provider.

## What is it?

According to training.gov.au, the qualification prepares workers to provide assistance to health professional staff in the care of clients, working directly with clients under supervision. The current version is Release 5 (July 2023).

## How the course is structured

- **15 units in total: **7 core units and 8 electives.
- **Core topics include: **communication, working with diverse people, body systems, infection control, workplace health and safety, medical terminology and organising your own work.
- **Specialisations: **for example Operating Theatre Technician, or Assisting in Nursing Work in Acute Care (which needs at least 80 hours of documented work experience).
- Other electives relate to areas like aged care, disability support, mental health, food services and clinical cleaning.

Exact units, placement and timetables depend on the school you choose.

## Who might it suit?

- People who are curious about working in hospitals or other health settings.
- People who like helping others and working as part of a team.
- Working Holiday Makers who want a practical qualification (check the study limit in your visa conditions, see [the 4-month rule](https://www.weintervisa.com/post/can-you-study-on-a-working-holiday-visa-417-462-the-4-month-rule-explained)).
