Questions Working Holiday Makers ask us

Can I study on a working holiday visa?

Yes. Home Affairs says Working Holiday Makers can study or train for up to 4 months on each visa (condition 8548). Check your own conditions in VEVO ↗.

How long are the fast-track courses?

It depends on the provider, the course and the intake. We send you the provider's current timetable in writing before you apply, so you can check it against your visa dates.

How much does it cost?

Fees depend on the course and intake. Ask us for a free written quote with the next intake dates.

What English level do I need?

It's set by the provider. Tell us your level (or test score) and we'll check. English support may be available.

Can I work while I study?

Your work rights come from your visa. Everyone working in Australia has the same workplace rights, including minimum pay and payslips.

  • Fair Work: visa holders' rights ↗
  • The 6-month employer limit explained
  • Specified work for a second or third visa (Home Affairs) ↗
Will a course get me a job, a second visa or PR?

No course guarantees a job, a visa or PR. We're an education agency working with registered migration partners, and can refer visa questions to a registered migration agent.
