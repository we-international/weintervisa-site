A partnership built carefully

Start small.
Build trust together.

We welcome applications from people who can make thoughtful introductions and communicate clearly with students.

Submitting an application does not activate a partner account. We review each application, discuss suitability and confirm approval before access is granted.

Partners introduce students and support communication. Preliminary checks are not a visa guarantee. Partners do not provide visa advice on our behalf.
What happens after I apply?

Your application enters a review queue. Our team may contact you for more information. The owner makes the approval decision before partner access is enabled.

How are referrals identified?

Approved partners will have their own referral reference and link. The student confirms the introduction, and our team checks existing records before assigning a case.

How does compensation work?

Compensation arrangements are confirmed individually in writing before they apply. Tuition payments, funds actually received by our company, cancellations and refunds are considered during review. No rate or payment date is promised on this page.

Can I send student documents now?

Please do not include student details or documents in your partner application. Student information must follow the consent and referral process provided after approval.
