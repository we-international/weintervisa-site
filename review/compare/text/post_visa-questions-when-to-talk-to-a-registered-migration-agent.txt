Visa Questions? When to Talk to a Registered Migration Agent
We International
2 days ago
1 min read

We help students compare courses and enrol. But we're an education agency, not a migration agent, so we don't give visa advice. Here's when it makes sense to talk to a registered migration agent.

When to get professional visa advice

You want to change visas, for example from a Working Holiday visa to a Student visa.

Your visa is close to expiring, or you're not sure which conditions apply to you.

You've had a visa refused or cancelled, or you've received a letter from Home Affairs.

Your situation is complicated (family members, previous visas, health or character questions).

Recent changes to keep in mind

From 2 October 2026, most temporary visa holders, including Working Holiday Makers, can no longer apply for a Student visa while in Australia. Read our summary: Student visa changes from 2 October 2026.

Check before you choose an adviser

In Australia, people who give immigration advice must be registered migration agents (or Australian legal practitioners). You can check a migration agent's registration on the Office of the Migration Agents Registration Authority (OMARA) register.

How we can help

For course questions, fees and intakes, send us an enquiry. For visa advice, see our Migration Support page. We can refer you to a registered migration agent.

This article is general information only, not migration advice. We can't guarantee any visa outcome.
