OUR STORY · SINCE 2023

Started in Sydney in 2023. Built around students.

We International Education & Visa began in Sydney in 2023 with one simple goal: to give international students honest, easy-to-understand help with studying in Australia.

Today we work with registered education providers (CRICOS) and registered migration agent partners, and our team includes a PIER-certified Qualified Education Agent Counsellor.
