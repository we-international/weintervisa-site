Homestay · with our partner AHN

New to Australia? Start with a homestay.

Your first weeks are easier with a local host family. Australian Homestay Network (AHN) matches you with a screened host near your school, with meals, bills and 24/7 emergency support included.

  • Hosts screened with background checks, in-home interviews and home inspections
  • Private furnished room, meals, Wi-Fi, utilities and laundry in one weekly price
  • Staffed 24/7 emergency contact centre with interpreters
  • Up to 1 hour from your school by public transport, in 17 cities and regions

Avoid accommodation scams

Don't pay a stranger for a room you've never seen

Some students send a bond or rent to someone online, then arrive in Australia and find there is no room. Booking a homestay through AHN helps you avoid this.

  • You pay AHN, an established homestay organisation — not a private person you found online
  • Your host and address are confirmed in a placement report before you fly
  • You can add airport pickup, so someone takes you straight to your new home
  • Stay for your first month, set up your bank account, phone and transport, and inspect rentals in person — then move out when you're ready

Planning to stay about 4 weeks? Tell AHN your plan when you apply and ask about the notice period.

🏡

Someone looks out for you

Your host helps with local customs, transport and your first trip to school, so you are not on your own on day one.

🛡️

Checked hosts and homes

AHN hosts pass background checks, an in-home interview and a home inspection, and complete host training. Hosts of under-18s are specially trained and certified.

📞

Help any time

A professionally staffed 24/7 critical-incident line with interpreters, plus your local AHN office in business hours.

🍽️

Meals sorted

Choose Complete (3 meals, 7 days), Traditional (2 meals weekdays, 3 on weekends), Dinner Only or No Meals. Special diets may cost extra.

💡

No surprise bills

The weekly fee covers your room, meal plan, utilities, internet, laundry, insurance, emergency support and administration. No bond and no lock-in contract.

🗺️

Time to find your feet

AHN recommends at least 4 weeks for new students. Most stay about 3 months, and you can extend while you look for your next place.

AHN says it was the first organisation endorsed under the Study NSW and NEAS Homestay Service Provider Standards (October 2023).

How it works

From sign-up to your first night

  1. Get your details ready: your school name and campus, study dates and flight details.
  2. Create your guest profile through the button on this page, with your preferences and dietary needs. Apply 2–4 weeks before you arrive (earlier gives you hosts closer to school).
  3. Pay your first invoice: the matching fee plus your first 4 weeks. Pay at least 3 weeks before arrival, because AHN can only match you once payment is received.
  4. AHN finds your host and sends you and your host a placement report. You can contact your host before you fly.
  5. Confirm your arrival. Add airport pickup if you want someone waiting for you.
  6. Settle in. After that, AHN invoices every 4 weeks.

Apply through the button on this page

The button opens AHN's guest application through our partner link. You fill in your details and send your request, then AHN's team matches you with a host family that suits you. Matching takes a little time, so apply 2–4 weeks before you arrive.

  • AHN knows you applied through us, so we can follow up with AHN for you
  • You pay AHN directly — no money goes to a private person
  • No need to sign up separately on AHN's own website — use the button here so we can look after you

Good to know

Living with a host family

Your stay

  • Minimum stay 4 weeks (28 nights)
  • After the first 4 weeks, give 2 weeks' written notice to AHN and your host
  • Pay by card, PayPal, AliPay or bank transfer

House etiquette

  • Tell your host when you go out, when you'll be back and if you'll miss a meal
  • Keep your room and bathroom tidy; showers 5–10 minutes
  • Ask before inviting visitors; quiet after 10pm
  • No charging e-bikes or power banks in the home

Under 18?

  • Specially trained and certified hosts
  • Home for dinner every night, no overnight stays away
  • Under-18 prices and a code of conduct apply
  • Guardianship is a separate package on request
FAQ

Common questions

What does the weekly fee include?

Your room, your meal plan, utilities, internet, laundry, insurance, 24/7 emergency support and administration. Internet data limits vary by household, so check with your host before streaming a lot.

Is there a one-off fee?

Yes, a matching fee: $350 in 2026 and $370 in 2027 (high school students may pay more where extra services are provided). It covers matching, host selection and training, home inspection and vetting.

Can I get a refund?

AHN refunds all fees if your visa is denied. Otherwise the matching fee is non-refundable once placement work has started. Read AHN's refund policy before you pay.

Will I have my own room?

Yes, a private furnished room with a bed, study area and wardrobe. Shared rooms cost less, and VIP homestay adds a private bathroom when available.

How far will I live from school?

AHN places students up to 1 hour from their education provider by public transport, and your host helps with your first trip.

Which cities are covered?

Adelaide, Brisbane, Canberra, Coffs Harbour, Darwin, Gold Coast, Hobart, Lismore, Melbourne, Newcastle & Central Coast, North Queensland, Perth, Regional WA, Sunshine Coast, Sydney, Toowoomba and Wollongong.

Can my partner or parents stay with me?

Parents can't stay in the homestay. Partners apply separately, ideally 2–3 months ahead.

Who do I contact once I'm placed?

AHN directly: 1300 024 628 or info@homestaynetwork.org. Their 24/7 emergency line details are given to you after registration. For help choosing a course or school, contact us.
