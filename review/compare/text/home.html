Common questions

Do you charge students for course advice?

Our course advice is free and there's no obligation. Ask us about any service before you start.

Can you help with my visa?

We're an education agency working with registered migration partners. We don't give visa advice ourselves, but we can refer you to a registered migration agent.

Can I study on a Working Holiday visa?

Yes. Home Affairs says Working Holiday Makers can study or train for up to 4 months on each visa (condition 8548). See our Working Holiday page.

Do you help students outside Sydney?

Yes. We support students online and can look at courses in other cities. Our office is in Sydney.

Can I talk to you in my language?

Yes. Use the "Read this page in your language" button to switch language, and tell us your preferred language when you contact us.
