We welcome applications from people who can make thoughtful introductions and communicate clearly with students.

Submitting an application does not activate a partner account. We review each application, discuss suitability and confirm approval before access is granted.

Partners introduce students and support communication. Preliminary checks are not a visa guarantee. Partners do not provide visa advice on our behalf.
What happens after I apply?

Your application enters a review queue. Our team may contact you for more information. The owner makes the approval decision before partner access is enabled.
