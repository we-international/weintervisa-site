THE REGISTRATION MAP
Check your pathway before booking a course.

Ahpra and the Nursing and Midwifery Board of Australia (NMBA) assess registration. We help you compare study and preparation options.

Check the applicable route

Eligible internationally qualified RNs may use the registration standard effective from 23 April 2025. Others follow the IQNM assessment process, beginning with Self-check.

IQRN registration standard ↗
Follow your Ahpra assessment

For the IQNM route, Self-check leads to a stream and required next steps. Complete the orientation and portfolio requirements that apply to you.

Steps after Self-check ↗
If required: NCLEX-RN, then OSCE

For relevant Stream B applicants, the multiple-choice examination comes before the clinical OSCE. Preparation courses can support study; they do not replace the official exams.

Official examination information ↗
Apply and meet all registration standards

Completing exams does not itself grant registration. NMBA assesses the application, including the applicable English-language and other registration standards.

Applying for registration ↗
A preparation course is not an Australian nursing qualification, registration approval or a visa pathway. Registration and visa requirements are separate.
